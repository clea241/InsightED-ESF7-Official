## Purpose

InsightED-ESF7 is a school reporting and compliance tool for the DepEd
ESF7 (Class Program Schedule) report. It helps schools generate,
manage, and submit ESF7 forms, and gives division/district offices
visibility into ESF7 status across schools.

## Target Users

- **School administrators/registrars** — prepare and manage ESF7
  reports at the school level.
- **Division/district office staff** — monitor and consolidate ESF7
  data across schools (rooms, clustering, progress).
- **Teachers** — teaching staff whose workload/schedule data feeds into
  the ESF7 report.
- **DepEd/central office reviewers** — review or approve submitted
  reports.

## Core Objectives

- **Automate ESF7 generation** — reduce manual work in producing ESF7
  class program schedules.
- **Workload & scheduling accuracy** — correctly compute teacher
  workload and avoid scheduling conflicts/overloads.
- **Monitoring dashboard** — give division-level visibility into
  schools' ESF7 status.
- **Data accuracy/compliance** — ensure submitted data meets DepEd ESF7
  formatting/compliance rules.
